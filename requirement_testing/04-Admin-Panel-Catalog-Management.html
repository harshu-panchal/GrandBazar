Zinto Manual Testing Guide
Part 4: Admin Panel — Catalog & Shop Management

Zinto Platform — Manual Requirement Testing Guide
This document walks you through checking that the Admin (Super Admin) side of Zinto works as expected — creating and managing sellers, adding or editing products on a seller's behalf, setting up a seller's shop end-to-end, organising categories, and configuring what customers see on the app's home page. Log in to the Admin Center with an admin/super-admin account and follow each numbered test below, in order, ticking off the Expected Result as you confirm it.

How to use this guide

Each test below has a number, a short title, and simple numbered steps telling you exactly what to click or tap. After the steps, an ✅ Expected Result line tells you exactly what you should see if everything is working correctly. Go through the tests one at a time, in your own copy of the Admin Center (usually reached from a web browser), and tick each one off once what you see matches the Expected Result. If a step doesn't apply because a screen looks slightly different, use your best judgement — the important thing is whether the end result matches.

4.3 Admin / Super Admin Panel

This section covers the main day-to-day admin tools: creating and onboarding sellers, setting business rules like commission and delivery charges, running the order lifecycle, and managing what appears on the app's home page.

Create a new seller (vendor) account Ref: Req #69

  1. In the left-hand menu, open Sellers → Active Sellers.
  2. Click the + Create Vendor Account button near the top of the page.
  3. Fill in the seller's name, shop details, email/phone, and password in the form that opens, then submit.
✅ Expected Result: The new seller appears in the Active Sellers list right away, with their shop name and contact details visible.

Send the new seller their login details by email Ref: Req #70

  1. Create (or open) a seller account as above.
  2. Look for an option such as "Send Credentials" or "Email Login Details" on the seller's account.
  3. Trigger it, then check the seller's registered email inbox.
✅ Expected Result: The seller receives an email containing their login ID/password and a link to the seller app or seller portal, so they can log in without calling support.

Manage a seller's onboarding/approval journey Ref: Req #71

  1. Go to Sellers → Seller Applications in the left menu.
  2. You will see sellers who have signed up and are waiting for review, grouped by status (e.g. Pending, Rejected/Deactivated).
  3. Open one application and review the shop details submitted.
  4. Use the Approve or Reject action on that seller's row.

✅ Expected Result: Approving moves the seller into Active Sellers and unlocks their ability to sell; rejecting moves them to the "Rejected / Deactivated" list with a reason recorded.

Manage subscription plans for sellers Ref: Req #72

  1. Go to Sellers → Subscriptions in the left menu.
  2. Review the list of subscription plans (e.g. plan name, price, billing cycle, number of shops/products allowed).
  3. Create a new plan or edit an existing one and save.
✅ Expected Result: The plan list updates with your changes, and this plan becomes selectable when setting up a seller's shop.

Configure commission rules Ref: Req #73

  1. For a city-wide rate, go to City Commissions in the left menu and set the commission percentage for a city.
  2. For a category-level rate, open Master Catalog or Products, edit a product, and turn on the "Apply Commission" option to set a specific commission for that item.
  3. For a single shop's rate, open Sellers → Active Sellers, click into a seller, go to their Shop Setup tab, and set "Apply Shop-wise Custom Commission Rate" under Plan & Monetization Setup.
✅ Expected Result: Each level (city, category/product, or individual shop) accepts and saves its own commission percentage or fixed amount, and orders from that scope are billed using the rate you set.

Manage orders through their full lifecycle Ref: Req #74

  1. Go to Orders → All Orders.
  2. Open any order to see its detail page.
  3. Follow the order through its stages — new, being prepared, on the way, delivered — using the status tabs (New Orders, Being Prepared, On the Way, Delivered, Cancelled, Returned) in the Orders menu.
✅ Expected Result: Every order can be tracked from placement to delivery (or cancellation/return) from this one Orders area, and its status tab always matches its real current stage.

Monitor every order across the platform Ref: Req #75

  1. Go to Orders → All Orders.
  2. Use the search box and filters (by status, seller, date) to look up any order placed on the platform, regardless of which seller it belongs to.
✅ Expected Result: You can find and view any order from any seller/store in one place, with accurate item, price, and status details.

Approve or reject customer cancellation requests Ref: Req #76

  1. Go to Orders → All Orders (or the relevant status tab) and look for an order flagged "Cancel Request Pending".
  2. Open that order's detail page — you will see a "Cancellation Request" section with the customer's reason.
  3. Click Approve to confirm the cancellation, or Reject with a note to keep the order active.
✅ Expected Result: Approving marks the order cancelled and (where applicable) starts a refund; rejecting keeps the order moving and notifies the customer with your note.

Assign a delivery rider after an order is packed Ref: Req #77

  1. Go to Orders → Being Prepared and find an order the seller has finished packing.
  2. Open the order, or go to Delivery Drivers → Track Drivers, and use Assign Delivery Partner.
  3. Pick an available rider from the list and confirm.

✅ Expected Result: The chosen rider is linked to the order and the order moves into "On the Way"; the rider's app shows the new delivery.

Manage the home page configuration Ref: Req #78

  1. Go to Marketing Tools → Create Sections (this opens the "Experience Studio" screen).
  2. Click Add Component and build a new home page section, or edit/reorder existing ones.
✅ Expected Result: Changes made here are reflected on the customer app's home page (check the Home Page vs Header Category Pages toggle at the top of the screen for each scope).

Manage banners, categories, featured products, offers, and seller highlights Ref: Req #79

  1. Open Marketing Tools → Create Sections (Experience Studio).
  2. Click Add Component and choose a type: Banners, Categories, Sub Categories, Products, Seller Highlights, or Super AD Placements.
  3. Fill in the heading and the items for that section (e.g. pick banners, or pick which products/categories/shops to feature) and save.
  4. Separately, check Marketing Tools → Offer Sections and Marketing Tools → Shop by Store for dedicated offer-card and themed-store screens.
✅ Expected Result: Each type of section (banner, category strip, featured products, offers, or seller highlights) can be created, edited, reordered, and shows correctly on the customer-facing app.

Manage seller recommendations for offline sellers Ref: Req #80

  1. Open Sellers → Active Sellers and click into any seller.
  2. Go to the Shop Setup tab and find Discovery Controls.
  3. Toggle "Exclude from 'Other stores near you' suggestions" on or off and save.
✅ Expected Result: When toggled on, this shop is never suggested as an alternative to customers when a nearby shop is closed; toggled off, it can appear in those suggestions.

Configure refunds by category Ref: Req #81

  1. Open Orders → Refund Ledger to review how refunds are currently processed.
  2. Check Master Catalog/category screens for any per-category refund/return-eligibility setting.
✅ Expected Result: An admin can set different refund rules (e.g. eligible or not, restocking fee) per product category, and refunds processed for that category follow the configured rule.

Configure odd-hour, weather, delivery and platform charges Ref: Req #82

  1. Go to Fees & Charges in the left menu.
  2. Turn on "Odd Hour Surcharge" and set its amount and time window (e.g. 10 PM to 6 AM).
  3. Turn on "Weather Surcharge" and set its amount.
  4. Review and adjust the standard delivery charge and platform charge fields on the same page, then save.
✅ Expected Result: All saved charges are applied to new customer orders under the matching condition (e.g. odd-hour surcharge only appears on orders placed in the configured night window).

Manage advertisement / Super AD placements Ref: Req #83

  1. Open Marketing Tools → Create Sections (Experience Studio).
  2. Click Add Component and choose the "Super AD Placements" type.
  3. Add one or more ad slots (image, link, seller) and save.
✅ Expected Result: The ad slot appears on the customer app in its designated placement and links to the correct product, shop, or offer.

Configure dynamic sections and platform-wide rules Ref: Req #84

  1. Open Settings and System Settings in the left menu to review platform-wide toggles.
  2. Open Marketing Tools → Create Sections to add or reorder dynamic home page sections.
✅ Expected Result: Platform-level rules and section layouts can be changed from these admin screens without needing a new app release, and the customer app reflects the change after refreshing.

Add or edit a seller's products on their behalf Ref: Req #85

  1. Go to Products in the left menu.
  2. Click + Add Product (or the equivalent "create" button).
  3. In the "Seller / Store" field, pick the seller this product belongs to.
  4. Fill in the product's General Info, Item Variants, Add-ons, and Media tabs, then save.
✅ Expected Result: The new product appears under the chosen seller's shop, exactly as if the seller had added it themselves.

Manage a seller's shop setup on their behalf Ref: Req #86

See the dedicated walkthrough of this flow under Section 12.4 — Full Shop Setup / Shop Management on Behalf of Seller further in this guide, which covers all the shop setup fields step by step.

View and manage seller settlement / bulk-order settlement Ref: Req #87

How this is confirmed: this capability is covered separately under the dedicated Seller Payments and Bulk Settlements screens (outside the scope of this catalog-focused guide) — no action needed here.

11. Product Catalog, Product Rules & Inventory Management

This section checks that products, their details, and stock behave correctly for customers shopping the app.

Seller-managed product details, images, pricing, stock and category Ref: Req #120

  1. Log in to a seller account (or, as admin, open Products and add a product for a chosen seller).
  2. Fill in name, description, images, price, sale price, stock quantity, and pick a category/subcategory.
  3. Save the product.
✅ Expected Result: All entered details are saved and show correctly on the product's page in the customer app, including the correct category/store association.

Product description details Ref: Req #121

  1. Open Products, edit any product, and go to the "General Info" tab.
  2. Update the Description field and save.
✅ Expected Result: The updated description shows on the product's detail page in the customer app.

Out-of-stock products are hidden from customers Ref: Req #122

  1. Open Products, edit a product, and set its stock to 0 (or mark it inactive), then save.
  2. Open the customer app and browse to that product's category, or search for it by name.
✅ Expected Result: The out-of-stock product no longer appears in category listings, search results, or suggested/offer sections on the customer app.

Time-based category/product availability rules Ref: Req #123

  1. Open a seller's Shop Setup tab (under Sellers → Active Sellers → seller → Shop Setup) and look at "Scheduling & Future Orders".
  2. Turn on scheduling and set the delivery windows/time rules, then save.
✅ Expected Result: Products from that shop can only be ordered for delivery inside the configured time windows on the customer app.

12.1 Super Admin Visibility of Seller Product Listings

This section checks that admin can see every seller's products in one place, with full details.

View all products listed by every seller Ref: Req #124

  1. Go to Products in the left menu.
  2. Scroll the list — products are grouped and labelled by the seller/shop name they belong to (or "Admin" for platform-added items).
✅ Expected Result: Every seller's products are visible in this one screen, clearly grouped by their shop name.

Seller-wise product details, status, pricing, category, and stock Ref: Req #125

  1. On the Products page, use the search box and category filter to narrow the list.
  2. Click into any product to see its full details, including status (active/inactive), price, category, and stock in one panel.
✅ Expected Result: Every important detail about a product — who sells it, its status, price, category, and current stock — is visible without leaving this screen.

12.2 Product Entry by Super Admin on Behalf of Seller

This checks the flow where an admin creates products directly under a chosen seller's shop.

Select a seller and add a product under their shop Ref: Req #126

  1. Go to Products and click + Add Product.
  2. In the "Seller / Store" dropdown on the General Info tab, choose the seller/shop this product belongs to.
✅ Expected Result: A note under the dropdown confirms "This product will be created directly under the selected seller's store"; leaving it blank instead creates a generic platform product.

Enter full product details on behalf of the seller Ref: Req #127

  1. With a seller selected, fill in the product name, pricing, category/subcategory, stock, description, and images across the General Info, Item Variants, and Media tabs.
  2. Save the product.
✅ Expected Result: The product saves successfully with every field intact and is now visible under that seller's shop.

Publish or manage the listing for that seller Ref: Req #128

  1. After saving the product, find it in the Products list under the seller's name.
  2. Open it again and change its status (e.g. active/inactive) or update details, then save.
✅ Expected Result: Admin can continue to publish, unpublish, or edit this product at any time on the seller's behalf, and the change reflects instantly in the list and on the customer app.

12.3 Product Editing by Super Admin

This section checks each individual field an admin can edit on an existing product.

Edit a product's name and general details Ref: Req #129

  1. Open Products, click the edit (pencil) icon on any product.
  2. On the "General Info" tab, change the product Name and save.
✅ Expected Result: The updated name shows immediately in the Products list and on the customer app.

Edit a product's description Ref: Req #130

  1. Open the same product's edit screen, General Info tab.
  2. Update the Description field and save.
✅ Expected Result: The new description is reflected on the product's detail page in the customer app.

Edit a product's category/subcategory Ref: Req #131

  1. Open the product's edit screen and go to the "Category" tab.
  2. Choose a different category and/or subcategory, then save.
✅ Expected Result: The product moves to the newly chosen category/subcategory and appears there in the customer app.

Edit a product's pricing Ref: Req #132

  1. Open the product's edit screen, General Info tab.
  2. Change the Price and/or Sale Price fields and save.
✅ Expected Result: The customer app shows the updated price (and any discount) the next time the product page loads.

Edit stock/inventory details Ref: Req #133

  1. Open the product's edit screen, General Info tab.
  2. Change the Stock quantity and Low Stock Alert value, then save.
✅ Expected Result: Stock updates instantly; when stock hits 0 the product is hidden from the customer app as tested in Req #122.

Edit product images Ref: Req #134

  1. Open the product's edit screen and go to the "Media" tab.
  2. Replace the main image and/or add/remove gallery images, then save.
✅ Expected Result: The new images show on the product card and detail page in the customer app.

Edit product visibility / listing status Ref: Req #135

  1. Open the product's edit screen, General Info tab.
  2. Change the Status field between "Active" and "Inactive", then save.
✅ Expected Result: Setting a product to Inactive removes it from the customer app immediately; Active brings it back.

Edit future-order / advance-booking configuration Ref: Req #136

  1. Open the product's edit screen, General Info tab, and look for the "Pre-order Eligible" option.
  2. Turn it on or off and save.
✅ Expected Result: When enabled, the product becomes available for advance/future booking on the customer app; when off, it only supports normal ordering.

Edit add-on and packaging-related configuration Ref: Req #137

  1. Open the product's edit screen and go to the "Add-ons" tab. Select or remove add-on products and mark any as required.
  2. On the General Info tab, review the Packaging Charge field (this can override the seller's default packaging charge for this one item), then save.
✅ Expected Result: The chosen add-ons appear as options when a customer buys this product, and the packaging charge override applies only to this product's orders.

12.4 Full Shop Setup / Shop Management on Behalf of Seller

This section walks through the complete "Shop Setup" screen admin uses to configure a seller's shop from A to Z, as if setting it up for them.

Configure the shop/store profile details Ref: Req #138

  1. Go to Sellers → Active Sellers and click on a seller to open their detail page.
  2. Click the Shop Setup tab.
  3. Under "Store Basic Profile", fill in Shop Name, Category, Description, Address, Locality, City, Pincode, and Service Radius.
  4. Click Save Shop Setup.
✅ Expected Result: All profile fields save and appear correctly when the seller's storefront is viewed in the customer app.

Configure shop banners, media, and about-shop details Ref: Req #139

  1. On the same Shop Setup tab, scroll to "Storefront Media".
  2. Upload a Store Logo, add up to 5 Carousel Banners, and paste a Store Promotional Video URL if desired.
  3. Save.
✅ Expected Result: The logo, banners, and video all show correctly on the seller's storefront page in the customer app.

Configure the shop's category mapping Ref: Req #140

  1. On the Shop Setup tab, set the shop's business Category field (e.g. Fashion & Apparel, Pharmacy & Health, Beauty & Cosmetics, Pet Care, General Store).
  2. Save Shop Setup.
✅ Expected Result: The shop shows up correctly under that business category when customers browse shops by category on the app.

Configure product listing under the shop Ref: Req #141

  1. Open the seller's detail page and click the Products tab to see everything currently listed under this shop.
  2. Go to Products in the main menu, add a new product, and choose this seller in the "Seller / Store" field to list it under their shop.
✅ Expected Result: The Products tab on the seller's page always matches exactly what's listed under their shop on the customer app.

Configure commission / business setup for the shop Ref: Req #142

  1. On the Shop Setup tab, find "Plan & Monetization Setup".
  2. Choose Commission Based and set a custom rate, or choose Subscription Plan and pick a plan from the dropdown.
  3. Save.
✅ Expected Result: The chosen business model applies to this shop's future orders — either the custom commission rate is deducted, or the shop is billed under the selected subscription plan.

Complete the operational setup needed to make the shop live Ref: Req #143

  1. On the Shop Setup tab, complete every section in order: Store Basic Profile, Plan & Monetization Setup, Storefront Media, Discovery Controls, and Scheduling & Future Orders.
  2. Click Save Shop Setup at the top of the tab.
✅ Expected Result: A confirmation message ("Shop setup updated successfully on behalf of seller") appears, and the shop is now fully configured and visible to customers as an operating store.

12.5 Admin Override / Assisted Seller Management Use Case

This checks that both a seller managing their own shop and an admin managing it on their behalf are supported side by side.

Support both self-managed and admin-assisted seller management Ref: Req #144

  1. As admin, open a seller's Shop Setup tab (Sellers → Active Sellers → seller) and make a small change, e.g. update the shop description, then save.
  2. Separately, log in to the same seller's own seller-portal account and open their shop settings screen.
  3. Confirm the seller can see and further edit the same details themselves.
✅ Expected Result: Both the admin and the seller can view and edit the same shop profile — whichever one last saves a change is reflected everywhere, with no conflict or duplicate shop record.

Support for top-level categories Ref: Req #145

  1. Go to Categories → Header Categories.
  2. Click Add New Header, fill in a name (e.g. "Electronics"), pick colours/icon, and save.
✅ Expected Result: The new header category appears in the list and shows as a top-level tab/section on the customer app.

Support for main categories Ref: Req #146

  1. Go to Categories → Main Categories.
  2. Click Add New Category, give it a name (e.g. "Laptops"), link it to a Header Category, and save.
✅ Expected Result: The new main category shows nested correctly under its chosen header category on the customer app.

Support for subcategories Ref: Req #147

  1. Go to Categories → Sub-Categories.
  2. Click Add New Subcategory, give it a name (e.g. "Gaming Laptops"), link it to its parent Main Category, and save.
✅ Expected Result: The subcategory appears correctly nested under its parent category, both in the admin category list and on the customer app's category navigation.

Products can be tagged to categories Ref: Req #147 (product tagging)

  1. Open Products, edit any product, and go to the "Category" tab.
  2. Assign it a Header, Category, and Subcategory, then save.
✅ Expected Result: The product shows up correctly when a customer browses to that specific category/subcategory.

Shop category navigation Ref: Req #148

  1. On the customer app, open the Categories or Stores browsing screen.
  2. Tap a category and confirm it filters to shops/products that belong to that category.
✅ Expected Result: Tapping a category consistently narrows down the shops and products shown to that category only.

Packaging-related category structures Ref: Req #149

  1. Open a category (Header, Main, or Sub) for editing and look for a packaging charge field.
  2. Set a value and save — then check that a product under that category without its own override uses this rate.
✅ Expected Result: The category-level packaging charge applies to products in that category unless a product has its own override (see Req #137).

Category model supports name, image, subcategories, and slug Ref: Req #150

  1. Open Categories → Header Categories (or Main/Sub), and edit any category.
  2. Confirm it has fields for Name, an uploaded Image, an auto-generated or editable URL Slug (e.g. "electronics"), and a list of its subcategories.
✅ Expected Result: All four pieces of information — name, image, slug, and subcategory list — are present and saved correctly for every category.

22. Home Page Configuration & CMS-Style Admin Control

This section checks that the customer app's home page can be fully controlled from the admin side without needing a developer.

Home page sections are dynamic and admin-configurable Ref: Req #218

  1. Go to Marketing Tools → Create Sections (Experience Studio).
  2. Add, reorder (using the up/down arrows on a section), or delete a section, then open the customer app's home page.
✅ Expected Result: The home page on the customer app reflects your section order and content changes without needing an app update.

Admin can manage banners Ref: Req #219

  1. In Experience Studio, click Add Component and choose "Banners".
  2. Upload one or more banner images, set their links, and save.
✅ Expected Result: The banners appear in the customer app's home page carousel and open the correct link when tapped.

Admin can manage categories shown on the home page Ref: Req #220

  1. In Experience Studio, click Add Component and choose "Categories" (or "Sub Categories").
  2. Pick which categories to show and how many rows, then save.
✅ Expected Result: The chosen categories appear on the home page in the layout configured.

Admin can manage product sections Ref: Req #221

  1. In Experience Studio, click Add Component and choose "Products".
  2. Give the section a heading, pick the products, set rows/columns (or single-row scroll), and save.
✅ Expected Result: A new product carousel/grid with your chosen heading and products appears on the home page.

Admin can manage featured products Ref: Req #222

  1. Open Products, edit a product, and turn on its "Featured" flag, then save.
  2. Confirm the product shows up in any home page section built to show featured items.
✅ Expected Result: Featured products are marked and surfaced correctly wherever the app highlights featured items.

Admin can manage offers Ref: Req #223

  1. Go to Marketing Tools → Offer Sections (or the Offers screen).
  2. Click Create Offer, fill in the offer title, short description, code, and discount, then save.
✅ Expected Result: The new offer card shows on the customer app and, where applicable, applies its discount at checkout.

Admin can manage seller highlights Ref: Req #224

  1. In Experience Studio, click Add Component and choose "Seller Highlights".
  2. Pick which shops to feature and save.
✅ Expected Result: The featured shops appear in a "Seller Highlights" section on the customer app's home page.

31.1 Offline Seller Scenario

This checks what a customer sees when their preferred shop is closed.

Customer sees alternative seller suggestions when a shop is offline Ref: Req #345

  1. On the customer app, open a shop's page for a store that is currently closed (marked "Currently Closed").
  2. Scroll down on that shop's page.
✅ Expected Result: An "Other stores near you" section appears with nearby open shops the customer can order from instead.

31.2 Admin Role (Seller Availability)

This checks the admin-side control over which shops can be suggested as alternatives.

Admin manages alternate-seller suggestions Ref: Req #346

  1. Go to Sellers → Active Sellers, click into a seller, and open their Shop Setup tab.
  2. Under "Discovery Controls", toggle "Exclude from 'Other stores near you' suggestions" on or off, then save.
  3. Confirm the change by testing Req #345 above — check whether this shop appears in another closed shop's suggestion list.
✅ Expected Result: A shop marked "Excluded" never appears as a suggested alternative to customers, giving admin full control over which shops participate in this feature.
