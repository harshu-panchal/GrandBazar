Zinto Manual Testing Guide — Part 3: Seller Panel

Zinto Platform — Manual Requirement Testing Guide
This document walks a non-technical reviewer through every seller-related feature of the Zinto platform, one small test at a time. It covers logging into the Seller Panel, managing products and stock, processing orders, publishing future/pre-book items, and viewing settlements. Log in as a seller (and, for a small number of tests, as the admin) on the Zinto web app and follow each numbered test below in order, ticking off the Expected Result as you go.

How to use this guide

Each test below has a number and a short, plain-language title. Follow the numbered steps exactly as written, in the app, on your own device. After the steps there is an "✅ Expected Result" line — if what you see on your screen matches that line, the test passes; tick it off on your printed copy. If a step mentions a screen or button name, look for that exact wording in the app. A few tests are marked "How this is confirmed" instead of steps — these describe behaviour that happens automatically in the background rather than something you click through.

4.2 Seller Panel

This section covers the core, everyday tools a seller uses: logging in, running the shop profile, managing products and stock, and processing customer orders from start to finish.

Test 1 — Logging in to the Seller Panel (Ref: Req #52)

  1. Open the Zinto seller web address in your browser.
  2. Enter your registered seller mobile number or email and password on the login screen.
  3. Tap/click the login button.
✅ Expected Result: You land on the Seller Panel Dashboard, showing your shop's summary (orders, sales, etc.) and a left-hand menu with Products, Stock, Orders, and other sections.

Test 2 — Managing your shop/store profile (Ref: Req #53)

  1. From the left-hand menu, click Profile.
  2. Review or update your shop details (name, address, contact info, etc.) shown on this screen.
  3. Make a small change (e.g. a contact number) and save it.
✅ Expected Result: Your change is saved and reflected immediately when you reopen the Profile screen.

Test 3 — Adding and managing products (Ref: Req #54)

  1. Click Products in the left-hand menu.
  2. Click the button to add a new product, fill in the product name, description, image, category, and price, then save.
  3. Confirm the new product now appears in your Products list, and try editing or removing it.
✅ Expected Result: The product is created, appears in your product list, and can be edited or deleted afterwards.

Test 4 — Managing your product listing (Ref: Req #55)

  1. On the Products screen, use the search box or category filters to find an existing product.
  2. Open it and toggle it active/inactive (or edit its details) to control whether it is visible to customers.
✅ Expected Result: The product's visibility/details update instantly and the change is reflected in your listing.

Test 5 — Setting product pricing (Ref: Req #56)

  1. Open any product from the Products screen.
  2. Find the pricing fields (regular price and sale price) and enter/update the values.
  3. Save your changes.
✅ Expected Result: The new price is saved and shows correctly on the product card, both in the Seller Panel and on the customer app.

Test 6 — Managing stock/inventory (Ref: Req #57)

  1. Click Stock in the left-hand menu.
  2. Find a product and update its "Current Stock" quantity.
  3. Reduce a product's stock to a very low number (at or below its low-stock alert level) and refresh the screen.
✅ Expected Result: The stock quantity updates, and the product is flagged as "Low Stock" on the Stock screen once it drops below its alert level.

Test 7 — Publishing future-order / pre-book products (Ref: Req #58)

  1. Open a product on the Products screen and turn on the "Available for future/pre-order" option, then save.
  2. Go to Pre-Order Campaigns in the left-hand menu and create a new campaign, adding that product along with the sale window and delivery dates.
✅ Expected Result: Only products marked as pre-order-eligible can be added to a campaign, and the campaign is saved for customers to book ahead of the delivery date.

Test 8 — Viewing and processing incoming orders (Ref: Req #59)

  1. Click Orders in the left-hand menu.
  2. Look at the tabs across the top: All, Pending, Scheduled, Confirmed, Packed, Out for Delivery, Delivered, Cancelled.
  3. Open the Pending tab and click any order to view its full details.
✅ Expected Result: New customer orders appear under "Pending", and clicking one shows the items, customer details, and available actions.

Test 9 — Accepting orders (Ref: Req #60)

  1. On the Orders → Pending tab, open a new order.
  2. Click the Accept/confirm action (or use "Accept" on the bulk-select toolbar for multiple orders at once).
✅ Expected Result: The order moves out of "Pending" into "Confirmed", and a success message such as "order(s) accepted" appears.

Test 10 — Packing orders (Ref: Req #61)

  1. Open a Confirmed order.
  2. Click Mark as Packed.
✅ Expected Result: The order shows "Packed ✓" and moves to the Packed tab; the delivery rider is notified that the order is ready for pickup.

Test 11 — Suggesting replacement products (Ref: Req #62)

  1. Open an order that has not yet been delivered and click on one of its items.
  2. Click the option to mark that item as unavailable and suggest a replacement.
  3. Pick a replacement product from your catalog, confirm its price, add a reason (e.g. "Item unavailable"), and submit.
✅ Expected Result: A replacement request is sent for that item, and the customer is asked to approve or reject the suggested substitute.

Test 12 — Shifting delivery to the next day (Ref: Req #63)

  1. Open an order that is not yet delivered/cancelled.
  2. In the order details, find the Delivery Schedule box and click the calendar/Reschedule button.
  3. Pick tomorrow's date and a delivery window from the "Reschedule Delivery" pop-up, then confirm.
✅ Expected Result: A message "Order rescheduled" appears, and the order's delivery date now shows the new date you picked.

Test 13 — Cancelling orders with a reason (Ref: Req #64)

  1. Open an order and click Cancel.
  2. In the "Cancel Order" pop-up, type a reason of at least 10 characters explaining why (this is shown to the customer).
  3. Click Confirm Cancellation.
✅ Expected Result: The order moves to the Cancelled tab, and the reason you typed is visible to the customer.

Test 14 — Uploading a delivery-time proof photo (Ref: Req #65)

How this is confirmed: for orders fulfilled through the seller's own (self) delivery, the proof-of-delivery photo is captured and uploaded as part of the delivery-completion step on the delivery side of the app rather than a separate seller-panel screen — confirm by completing a self-delivered order end-to-end and checking that a delivery photo is attached to it in the order's history.

Test 15 — Managing future-order configuration for selected products (Ref: Req #66)

  1. Go to Pre-Order Campaigns and open (or create) a campaign.
  2. Add or remove products from the campaign's product list and adjust its dates.
✅ Expected Result: Only products individually marked "Available for future/pre-order" can be selected here, and your changes save against that campaign.

Test 16 — Viewing settlement-related data (Ref: Req #67)

  1. Click Earnings in the left-hand menu.
  2. Review your available balance, and the order-by-order table showing platform commission and packaging amounts.
✅ Expected Result: Your current available balance is shown, along with a breakdown of commission/fees for each order.

Test 17 — Operating with platform logistics or self-delivery mode (Ref: Req #68)

  1. Go to Delivery Policy in the left-hand menu.
  2. Check whether "Platform Logistics" and/or "Seller Delivery" (self-delivery) is switched on for your shop.
  3. Place a test order against your shop and confirm it is routed the way you configured (a delivery rider assigned by Zinto, or handled by your own delivery staff).
✅ Expected Result: Orders are fulfilled using whichever mode(s) are switched on for your shop in Delivery Policy.

5. Seller Onboarding & Vendor Management

This section covers how a new seller gets set up on Zinto — from the admin creating the account to the seller receiving their login details.

Admin sends invite (email)
→
Seller opens link & applies
→
Admin reviews application
→
Seller account approved
→
Seller logs in & sets up shop

Test 18 — Admin can create vendor/seller accounts (Ref: Req #88)

  1. Log in to the Admin Panel and open the "New Seller Applications" (Pending Sellers) screen.
  2. Click the button to invite a new seller and enter their email address (and phone, if asked).
  3. Send the invite.
✅ Expected Result: A confirmation message such as "Invite sent" appears, and the new invite shows up in the admin's list of sent invites.

Test 19 — Vendor login credentials shared through email (Ref: Req #89)

How this is confirmed: after the admin creates/approves a seller account, check the inbox of the email address used for that seller — a message with their login details (or a link to set a password) should arrive automatically, without the admin having to do anything further.

Test 20 — Seller application link shared through email (Ref: Req #90)

  1. After an admin sends a seller invite (Test 18), check the inbox of the email address that was invited.
  2. Open the email and click the web link inside it.
✅ Expected Result: The link opens the Zinto seller application/sign-up page in a browser, ready for the new seller to fill in their details.

Test 21 — Admin can manage vendor accounts and onboarding centrally (Ref: Req #91)

  1. In the Admin Panel, open "New Seller Applications" to review sellers awaiting approval, and "Active Sellers" to see approved ones.
  2. Approve or reject a pending application, and confirm you can view/manage an already-active seller's account from the Active Sellers screen.
✅ Expected Result: The admin can see every seller's status (pending or active) in one place and take action on each from these two screens.

6. Seller Subscription Model & Seller Limitation

This section covers the product-limit rules for sellers and how they can choose a subscription plan or a commission-based model.

Test 22 — Up to 30 products under the base plan (Ref: Req #92)

  1. As a seller who has not chosen a subscription plan, go to Products and add products one by one, keeping count.
  2. Continue adding until you reach 30 products.
✅ Expected Result: You can freely add products up to 30 without being asked to subscribe.

Test 23 — Must choose a subscription plan to add more than 30 products (Ref: Req #93)

  1. With 30 products already listed, try to add a 31st product.
✅ Expected Result: You are stopped and prompted to choose a monthly subscription plan (via Subscription in the left-hand menu) before you can add more products.

Test 24 — Higher commission if no subscription is chosen (Ref: Req #94)

  1. As a seller who has stayed on the free/base plan at the 30-product limit, place a test order for one of your products.
  2. Check the commission shown for that order on the Earnings screen and compare it with a seller who is on a paid subscription plan.
✅ Expected Result: Sellers who have not taken a subscription plan are charged a higher commission rate than subscribed sellers.

Test 25 — Choosing subscription-basis or commission-basis model (Ref: Req #95)

  1. During onboarding (or from your account settings), open the "Choose your business model" screen.
  2. Review the two cards: Commission model (no upfront fee, commission per order) and Subscription model (pay a plan fee).
  3. Select one and confirm.
✅ Expected Result: Your shop is activated under the model you picked, and you can see this reflected in how orders are charged afterwards.

18. Seller Logistics Flexibility / Logistics Participation Model

This section covers whether a seller must use Zinto's own delivery riders, or can deliver orders themselves, or both.

Test 26 — Seller is not forced to use platform logistics (Ref: Req #184)

  1. Go to Delivery Policy in the seller left-hand menu.
  2. Confirm you can switch off "Platform Logistics" for your shop if you prefer to deliver yourself.
✅ Expected Result: Platform Logistics can be turned off without blocking you from taking orders — you are not locked into using Zinto's riders.

Test 27 — Platform-assigned logistics mode (Ref: Req #185)

  1. On Delivery Policy, turn on "Platform Logistics" and save.
  2. Place a test order for your shop and pack it (Test 10).
✅ Expected Result: Once packed, the order is picked up and delivered by a Zinto-assigned delivery rider.

Test 28 — Seller self-delivery mode (Ref: Req #186)

  1. On Delivery Policy, turn on "Seller Delivery" (self-delivery) and save.
  2. Place a test order for your shop and pack it.
✅ Expected Result: The order is fulfilled by your own delivery staff rather than a Zinto rider.

Test 29 — Hybrid / configurable logistics model (Ref: Req #187)

  1. On Delivery Policy, switch on both "Platform Logistics" and "Seller Delivery" together, and save.
✅ Expected Result: Both options can be enabled at the same time, letting your shop use a mix of platform riders and your own delivery.

Test 30 — Admin can configure a seller's logistics mode (Ref: Req #188)

  1. Log in as admin, open the seller's account under Active Sellers.
  2. Look for the option to control that seller's logistics mode (platform, self, or both) from the admin side.
✅ Expected Result: An admin can view and adjust which logistics mode(s) a given seller is allowed/set to use, independent of the seller's own setting.

Test 31 — Admin assigns logistics after seller packing (Ref: Req #189)

  1. As a seller using platform logistics, pack an order (Test 10).
  2. Log in as admin and open the Delivery Management / assignment screen for that order.
  3. Assign a delivery rider to the packed order.
✅ Expected Result: Once the seller marks the order as packed, it becomes available for the admin to assign to a delivery rider.

Test 32 — Seller-managed fulfillment in self-delivery mode (Ref: Req #190)

  1. With "Seller Delivery" switched on, pack a test order.
  2. Confirm the order's status moves through to "Out for Delivery" and "Delivered" without needing an admin-assigned rider.
✅ Expected Result: The whole delivery journey for that order is handled and updated by the seller's own team, end to end.

Test 33 — Seller views incoming orders (Ref: Req #265)

Test 34 — Seller accepts and processes orders (Ref: Req #266)

  1. Open a Pending order and click Accept.

Test 35 — Seller packs orders (Ref: Req #267)

  1. Open a Confirmed order and click Mark as Packed.
✅ Expected Result: The order status changes to Packed and shows "Packed ✓".

Test 36 — Seller may suggest replacement products (Ref: Req #268)

  1. Open any active order, choose an item, and use the option to mark it unavailable and suggest a replacement product.
✅ Expected Result: A replacement suggestion is sent to the customer for approval.

Test 37 — Seller may shift an order to next day (Ref: Req #269)

  1. Open an active order, click the reschedule/calendar button next to "Delivery Schedule", and pick the next day's date and a delivery window.

Test 38 — Seller uploads/captures delivery-time proof photo (Ref: Req #270)

How this is confirmed: for a self-delivered order, complete the delivery and check that a photo is captured/attached at the moment of delivery, confirming the order reached the customer.

Test 39 — Seller can cancel order with a valid reason (Ref: Req #271)

  1. Open an order and click Cancel.
  2. Enter a cancellation reason (at least 10 characters) and confirm.
✅ Expected Result: The order is cancelled and your reason is recorded and shown to the customer.

Test 40 — Fulfilling through platform logistics or self-delivery (Ref: Req #272)

  1. Check your Delivery Policy setting, then pack a test order and follow it through to delivery.
✅ Expected Result: The order is fulfilled using whichever mode (platform rider or your own delivery staff) is configured for your shop.

26.2 Seller Future Order Panel

This section covers the dedicated screen where a seller publishes products for future/pre-booked delivery.

Test 41 — Dedicated Future Order / Pre-Booking panel (Ref: Req #274)

  1. Click Pre-Order Campaigns in the left-hand menu.
  2. Click the button to create a new campaign, giving it a title, description, and status.
✅ Expected Result: A dedicated Pre-Order Campaigns screen opens where you can create and manage future-dated product listings, separate from your regular Products screen.

26.3 Future Product Configuration by Seller

This section covers the specific settings a seller can configure for a future/pre-book product and its campaign.

→
Create campaign & add product
→
Set sale window & delivery dates
→

Test 42 — Marking a product as Future Order / Pre-Book (Ref: Req #275)

  1. Open a product on the Products screen.
  2. Turn on "Available for future/pre-order" and save.
✅ Expected Result: The product is now eligible to be added to a Pre-Order Campaign.

Test 43 — Configuring the future delivery date (Ref: Req #276)

  1. In Pre-Order Campaigns, open or create a campaign.
  2. Set the Delivery Start Date field to the date customers will receive the product.
✅ Expected Result: The chosen delivery date is saved with the campaign and shown to customers when they book.

Test 44 — Configuring the future delivery window (Ref: Req #277)

  1. In the same campaign, set the Delivery End Date and/or the delivery windows for the campaign.
✅ Expected Result: The delivery window (start-to-end range) is saved and applied to orders placed under this campaign.

Test 45 — Configuring the booking availability period (Ref: Req #278)

  1. In the campaign form, set Sale Start and Sale End dates/times — this is the window during which customers may place a booking.
✅ Expected Result: Customers can only book the product between the sale-start and sale-end times you configured.

Test 46 — Configuring the applicable scheduling range (Ref: Req #279)

  1. In the campaign form, set "Max Advance Booking Days" — the number of days ahead a customer is allowed to book.
✅ Expected Result: Customers cannot book delivery further ahead than the number of days you set here.

27.4 Seller/Admin Cancellation

This section confirms that both sellers and admins are able to cancel orders, always with a stated reason.

Test 47 — Seller and Admin can cancel orders (Ref: Req #291)

  1. As a seller, open an order in Orders and click Cancel to cancel it.
  2. Separately, log in as admin, open the same/another order under Order Management, and cancel it from there too.
✅ Expected Result: Both the seller and the admin are able to cancel an order from their own respective panels.

Test 48 — Seller must provide a valid cancellation reason (Ref: Req #292)

  1. Click Cancel on an order.
  2. Try to confirm cancellation while leaving the reason box empty or with only a few characters.
  3. Then type a proper reason (10+ characters) and confirm.
✅ Expected Result: The cancellation is blocked until a reason of at least 10 characters is entered; the "CONFIRM CANCELLATION" button stays disabled until then.

28.1 Shift Order to Next Day

This section covers a seller's ability to push a delivery to the next day when a product cannot go out today.

Test 49 — Shift delivery date to next day (Ref: Req #293)

  1. Open an active order that has not yet been delivered.
  2. Click the reschedule (calendar) button beside "Delivery Schedule".
  3. Choose tomorrow's date and a delivery window, add an optional note explaining why, then click Confirm.
✅ Expected Result: The order's delivery date shows the new (next) day, and the customer is informed of the updated delivery date.

28.5 Delivery Extension

This section covers a seller being able to push delivery further out, beyond just the next day, when needed.

Test 50 — Extending the delivery date (Ref: Req #302)

  1. Open an active order and click the reschedule (calendar) button.
  2. In the "Reschedule Delivery" pop-up, pick any later date allowed (up to the maximum days-ahead configured for your shop) and a delivery window.
  3. Confirm the change.
✅ Expected Result: The order's delivery date is pushed out to the new date you selected, and the customer sees the updated delivery date on their order.

30.5 Seller Settlement Visibility

This section covers exactly what settlement information a seller can see for their orders — amounts, deductions, history, and status.

Test 52 — Seller panel shows total payable amount (Ref: Req #330)

  1. Open Earnings.
  2. Look for your overall available/payable balance shown at the top of the screen.
✅ Expected Result: A clear total amount currently payable/available to you is displayed.

Test 53 — Seller panel shows order-wise settlement amount (Ref: Req #331)

  1. On Earnings, scroll to the order-by-order table.
  2. Check that each row shows the settlement amount for that specific order.
✅ Expected Result: Every delivered order has its own line with the amount settled/settleable for that order.

Test 54 — Seller panel shows commission deductions (Ref: Req #332)

  1. On Earnings, check the "Commission & Fees" column for each order.
✅ Expected Result: The platform commission (and any packaging fee) deducted for that order is clearly shown alongside the order.

Test 55 — Seller panel shows settlement history (Ref: Req #333)

  1. Click Payment History (Transactions) in the left-hand menu.
✅ Expected Result: A full, searchable history of past settlements/payments is available to review, not just the current balance.

Test 56 — Seller panel shows settlement status (Ref: Req #334)

  1. On Earnings or Payment History, check the status label next to each order/payment (for example: Pending, Settled, On Hold).
✅ Expected Result: Each settlement clearly shows its current status, so you always know whether money is pending, on hold, or already settled.
