Zinto Manual Testing Guide — Part 2: Search, Discovery & Notifications

Zinto Platform — Manual Requirement Testing Guide

This document walks you through checking every search, discovery and notification feature of the Zinto app, one small test at a time. No technical knowledge is needed — just the app open in front of you and this guide by your side. It is meant to be used by anyone on the client side who wants to confirm, screen by screen, that these features genuinely work as expected before sign-off.

How to use this guide

Each test below has a number and a short title. Follow the numbered steps exactly, in order, using the real Zinto customer app. After each test, read the "✅ Expected Result" line and check whether what you saw on your screen matches it. If it matches, tick it off as working. If it doesn't, make a note of what happened instead so it can be reviewed. A few tests are backend behaviours that can't be "clicked through" directly — for those you'll see a one-line "How this is confirmed" note instead of steps.

14.1 Search Types Supported

This section covers the different ways a customer can search for what they want — typing, speaking, searching by shop, or browsing by category.

Search for a product by typing a name Ref: Req #151

  1. Open the Zinto app and go to the Home screen.
  2. Tap the search bar at the top (it shows a typing animation like "Search 'bread'", "Search 'milk'").
  3. Type the name of any product you know is sold on the app, for example "biscuit".
✅ Expected Result: As you type, matching products appear on the screen below the search bar, showing their picture, name and price.

Get instant suggestions while typing Ref: Req #152

  1. On the Search screen, start typing a product name slowly, one or two letters at a time.
  2. Pause after each couple of letters and watch the results area.
✅ Expected Result: You don't need to finish typing or press any button — the list of matching products updates on its own within a second of you pausing, showing suggestions that match what you've typed so far.

See your past searches saved Ref: Req #153

  1. Search for a product, for example "milk", and wait for results to appear.
  2. Tap the back arrow or clear the search box, then tap the search bar again to open the Search screen fresh.
✅ Expected Result: A "Recently Searched" row appears near the top of the empty Search screen, showing "milk" as a small pill you can tap to search it again, along with a small "X" to remove it.

See trending / popular searches Ref: Req #154

  1. Open the Search screen without typing anything.
  2. Look below the "Recently Searched" row (if any) for a "Trending Searches" row.
✅ Expected Result: A row titled "Trending Searches" shows a set of popular search terms as tappable pills; tapping one instantly searches for that term. (If your account or area has no trending data yet, this row may not appear — that itself should be checked with the team.)

Search using your voice Ref: Req #155

  1. On the Search screen, tap the microphone icon inside the search box.
  2. Allow microphone access if your browser/device asks for permission.
  3. Say a product name clearly, for example "chocolate".
✅ Expected Result: A "Listening…" popup appears with an animation while you speak, then closes on its own and the search box fills in with the word you said, showing matching results.

Voice search correctly turns speech into text Ref: Req #156

  1. Repeat the voice search steps above, this time saying a longer phrase such as "coconut oil".
  2. Watch the search box while you are speaking.
✅ Expected Result: The words you say appear as typed text in the search box in real time as you speak them, and once you stop, that exact text is used to search and is also saved to "Recently Searched".

Voice search shows a clear "listening" screen Ref: Req #157

  1. Tap the microphone icon to start voice search.
  2. While the popup is open, notice the animation, the "Listening…" title, and the "Tap to stop" button.
  3. Tap "Tap to stop" (or tap outside the popup) to stop listening early.
✅ Expected Result: A full popup appears clearly showing that the app is listening (with a moving animation and a live preview of what it's hearing), and tapping "Tap to stop" or the background closes it immediately.

Search directly for a seller/shop by name Ref: Req #158

  1. On the Search screen, type the name of a shop/store you know is on the app.
  2. Look at the results above the product list.
✅ Expected Result: A "Shops & Stores" section appears at the top of the results showing matching stores with their logo, name, category and distance from you; tapping one opens that store's page.

Browse and search stores directly (not just products) Ref: Req #159

  1. From the Home screen, tap "Stores" in the bottom navigation bar (or tap the store icon at the top on a larger screen).
  2. On the Stores screen, use the search box at the top to type a shop name or category.
✅ Expected Result: The Stores screen lists nearby shops as cards; typing in its search box narrows the list down to matching shops only.

Search for a seller's products specifically Ref: Req #160

  1. Open a specific store from the Stores screen or from a search result.
  2. Look for a way to search only within that store's own product list.
✅ Expected Result: There should be a search box on the store's own page that only searches that seller's products. If no such search box exists on the store page, this should be flagged to the team as missing.

Search by category name Ref: Req #161

  1. On the Search screen, tap one of the category filter pills shown just below the search bar (e.g. "Fruits & Vegetables", "Dairy").
✅ Expected Result: The pill highlights and the product list below updates to show only products from that category, even with the search box left empty.

Browse categories using a grid of pictures Ref: Req #162

  1. Tap "Category" in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Look at how categories are displayed on this screen.
✅ Expected Result: The Categories screen shows category groups, each with a grid of picture tiles (icon + name) that you can tap to open that category's products.

Move from a category into its sub-categories Ref: Req #163

  1. From the Categories screen, tap any category tile.
  2. On the category's product page, look for tabs or a strip of sub-category options near the top.
✅ Expected Result: After opening a category, related sub-categories are shown as tabs or chips you can tap to further narrow down the products shown.

14.2 Smart Search & Recommendation Layer

This section checks that the app is "smart" about helping you find things — suggesting products and guiding discovery, not just returning plain keyword matches.

App shows smart product recommendations Ref: Req #164

  1. Open the Home screen and scroll down through the sections (e.g. "Lowest Price Ever!", featured product rows).
  2. Add a product to your cart and go to the Checkout screen.
✅ Expected Result: You see rows of recommended products on the Home screen and/or a "You might also like" section on the Checkout screen, tailored around what's popular or relevant, not a random fixed list.

Auto-suggestions appear as you type a search Ref: Req #165

  1. Open the Search screen and type a partial product name, e.g. "cho" for chocolate.
✅ Expected Result: Suggested products matching "cho" (such as chocolate, chocolate biscuits, etc.) appear automatically without needing to press Enter or a search button.

Discover products intelligently, not just by exact search Ref: Req #166

  1. On the Search screen, leave the search box empty and check the sections shown (Recently Searched, Trending Searches, Lowest Price Ever).
  2. On the Checkout screen, check the "You might also like" / "Perfect with your order" row.
✅ Expected Result: Even without typing anything, the app proactively shows you relevant products to discover — based on price, trends, or your order — rather than leaving the screen empty.

14.3 Search Results Behaviour

This section checks how different types of results (stores, products, categories) are combined and displayed together on the Search screen.

Stores and products both show up together in one search Ref: Req #167

  1. On the Search screen, type a word that could match both a shop name and a product name, e.g. a common grocery term.
  2. Look at the layout of the results screen.
✅ Expected Result: The results page shows a "Shops & Stores" section and a separate "Products" section on the same screen at the same time, each with its own count of matches, rather than forcing you to search separately for shops and products.

15. Map-Based Search & Discovery

This section checks whether customers can find and discover stores visually on a map, in addition to plain lists.

Find nearby stores using a map, sorted by distance Ref: Req #168

  1. From the Home screen, scroll to the "Stores near you" section and tap it, or tap "Stores" in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. On the Stores screen, tap the "Map View" button near the top.
  3. Use the distance filter dropdown to narrow results to a smaller radius (e.g. within 2 km).
✅ Expected Result: A map opens showing pins for each nearby store and a pin for your own location; switching the distance filter changes which stores are shown, and each store card also lists its area/city so you can tell how far or in which locality it is.

Home screen shows store visibility on a map-style preview Ref: Req #169

  1. On the Home screen, scroll down until you reach the "Stores near you" section.
  2. Look at the preview shown — it should resemble a small map with store markers.
  3. Tap "View all" to confirm it takes you to the full Stores/Map screen.
✅ Expected Result: The Home screen shows a map-style teaser card with pins for nearby stores; tapping it (or "View all") opens the full interactive Stores map. Note: this feature is still being finished, so minor rough edges here are expected — the goal of this test is simply to confirm the map teaser appears and links through correctly.

16. Recommendation, Add-On & Cross-Sell Module

This section checks that the app suggests sensible extra items to go along with what a customer is already buying (like suggesting a torch when buying an umbrella).

Get smart add-on suggestions that pair with your order Ref: Req #170

  1. Add a product to your cart, for example a snack item, from any store.
  2. Go to the Checkout screen.
  3. Scroll through the checkout page and look for a row titled "Perfect with your order" (marked "Seller Recommends") or "You might also like".
✅ Expected Result: A horizontal row of suggested products appears on the checkout screen that logically pairs with what's already in your cart (for example, a matching snack or accessory), and you can tap "Add" on any of them directly from that row.

Add-on products can be priced, charged commission on, and managed like normal products Ref: Req #171

How this is confirmed: this checks how add-on products are set up and billed behind the scenes (pricing, commission, GST) rather than something you click through as a customer. To confirm it, ask the seller/admin team to open a seller's product catalog, add or view an "add-on" style product, and verify it is priced and taxed the same way as any regular product when it appears in a customer's order.

Get notified when your order is placed Ref: Req #347

  1. Add a product to your cart and complete checkout to place a new order.
  2. Once the order confirmation screen appears, tap the profile icon (bottom navigation "Profile"), then tap the bell icon at the top right to open Notifications.
✅ Expected Result: A new notification appears at the top of the Notifications screen confirming your order was placed successfully, with a timestamp matching when you checked out.

Get notified when the seller accepts your order Ref: Req #348

  1. After placing an order, wait for the seller to accept it (or ask the seller/admin team to accept it from their side for this test).
  2. Check the Notifications screen again.
✅ Expected Result: A notification appears letting you know the seller has accepted/confirmed your order.

Get notified when your order is packed Ref: Req #349

  1. Once the seller marks your order as packed (ask the seller/admin team to move the order to "Packed" status if testing end-to-end), check the Notifications screen.
✅ Expected Result: A notification appears telling you your order has been packed and is ready for pickup/delivery.

Get notified when a delivery partner is assigned Ref: Req #350

  1. Once a delivery rider is assigned to your order (ask the admin/delivery team to assign one if testing end-to-end), check the Notifications screen.
✅ Expected Result: A notification appears confirming that a delivery partner has been assigned to bring your order to you.

Get notified when your order is out for delivery Ref: Req #351

  1. Once your order status moves to "Out for Delivery" (visible on the Order tracking screen under "Orders" in the bottom navigation), check the Notifications screen.
✅ Expected Result: A notification appears letting you know your order is now out for delivery and on its way.

Get notified when your order is delivered Ref: Req #352

  1. Once your order is marked as delivered, check the Notifications screen.
✅ Expected Result: A notification appears confirming your order has been delivered.

Get notified about cancellation updates Ref: Req #353

  1. Place an order and then cancel it (from "Orders" > open the order > Cancel), or request cancellation of an already-packed order.
  2. Check the Notifications screen after the cancellation is processed or reviewed.
✅ Expected Result: A notification appears confirming your cancellation was received and processed (or, if it was rejected, that it was reviewed and declined with a reason).

Get notified about refund updates Ref: Req #354

  1. Following a cancelled order or an approved return, wait for the refund to be initiated and then completed.
  2. Check the Notifications screen at each stage, and also check your Wallet balance under "Profile".
✅ Expected Result: You receive a notification when the refund is initiated, and another when it is completed, and the refunded amount reflects correctly in your wallet or original payment method.
