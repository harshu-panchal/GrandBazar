Zinto Manual Testing Guide — Part 1: Customer App (Shopping Experience)

Zinto Platform — Manual Requirement Testing Guide

This document is a simple, step-by-step checklist for confirming that the Zinto customer app works the way it should. It is written for anyone to use — no technical knowledge is needed. Log in as a customer on the Zinto app (on your phone or computer browser) and go through each numbered test below, one at a time, doing exactly what it says and checking whether you see the "Expected Result" described.

How to use this guide

  • Every test has a number and a short title telling you what it checks.
  • Follow the numbered steps exactly, in order, on the real Zinto customer app.
  • After finishing the steps, compare what you saw with the ✅ Expected Result box.
  • If what you see matches the Expected Result, tick that test off as working. If it does not match, make a note of what happened instead.
  • A few tests are not something you can tap through yourself — for those you'll see a one-line note titled "How this is confirmed" instead of steps.
  • Some tests involve several screens or people (for example, an order moving from you, to the seller, to the delivery partner). For those, a simple picture with boxes and arrows is shown first so you can see the whole journey before testing it.

4.1 Customer Panel / Customer App

This section covers the core shopping journey a customer goes through — from creating an account to placing, tracking, and reviewing an order.

Create an account or log in (Ref: Req #37)

  1. Open the Zinto customer app and go to the login screen.
  2. Enter your mobile number and tap "Continue" (or "Send OTP").
  3. Enter the 4-digit OTP code you receive and tap to verify.
  4. If you are a new user, fill in your name and tap the checkbox agreeing to the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy, then finish signing up.
✅ Expected Result: You are logged into your Zinto account and land on the Home screen with your name/profile visible.

Browse products and stores (Ref: Req #38)

  1. From the Home screen, scroll through the product categories and featured products.
  2. Tap the "Stores" section (or a category tile) to see a list of nearby stores.
  3. Tap on any store card to open that store's page and view its products.
✅ Expected Result: Products and stores load with images, names, and prices, and you can move between Home, category pages, and individual store pages smoothly.

Search for products and sellers (Ref: Req #39)

  1. Tap the search bar at the top of the Home screen.
  2. Type the name of a product (e.g. "milk") and watch the results appear.
  3. Clear the search and type the name of a store instead.
✅ Expected Result: Matching products or stores appear in the results list as you type, and tapping a result opens that product or store.

Add products to the cart (Ref: Req #40)

  1. Open any product from the Home screen or a store page.
  2. Tap the "Add" button (or the + icon) on the product.
  3. Tap the cart icon to open your Cart page and confirm the item is listed there.
✅ Expected Result: The item appears in your Cart with the correct name, price, and quantity, and the quantity can be increased or decreased from the Cart page.

Place an order (Ref: Req #41)

  1. With at least one item in your Cart, tap "Proceed to Checkout" (or similar) from the Cart page.
  2. Confirm or select your delivery address.
  3. Choose a payment method (e.g. Cash on Delivery, online payment, or Wallet).
  4. Tick the checkbox confirming you agree to the Return & Exchange Policy.
  5. Tap the final "Place Order" / "Pay" button (slide-to-pay on larger screens).
✅ Expected Result: You see an order confirmation screen, and the new order appears in "My Orders" with a status such as "Placed" or "Order Confirmed".

Schedule an order for a future time (Ref: Req #42)

  1. Go to the Checkout page as if placing a normal order.
  2. Look for the delivery timing option (a "Schedule" option that opens a date/time sheet).
  3. Pick a future date and time slot, then tap "Done".
  4. Complete the rest of checkout and place the order as usual.
✅ Expected Result: The order is placed with the future date/time you chose clearly shown, instead of being delivered immediately.

Track your order status in real time (Ref: Req #43)

  1. Go to "My Orders" and open any active (not yet delivered) order.
  2. Watch the order status area on the Order Details screen.
  3. Keep the screen open while the seller/delivery partner updates the order (e.g. from "Packed" to "Out for Delivery") and confirm the screen updates without you refreshing manually.
✅ Expected Result: The order status updates automatically as it changes — e.g. Placed → Accepted → Packed → Out for Delivery → Delivered — and, where available, you can see the delivery partner's live location on a map.

Cancel an order while it is still allowed (Ref: Req #44)

  1. Place a new order and immediately go to "My Orders" and open it.
  2. While the order is still waiting for the seller to accept it, tap "Cancel Order".
  3. Confirm the cancellation when asked.
✅ Expected Result: Because the seller had not yet accepted the order, it is cancelled immediately and its status changes to "Cancelled". A refund (if any amount was paid) is processed to your wallet or original payment method.

Raise a cancellation request on an order already accepted (Ref: Req #45)

  1. Open an order that the seller has already accepted (but no delivery partner has been assigned yet) from "My Orders".
  2. Tap "Cancel Order".
  3. Read the message explaining this will now be sent to the admin for approval, and confirm.
  4. Check the Order Details screen for a "Cancellation request is under admin review" note.
✅ Expected Result: Instead of cancelling instantly, you see a message that your request has been sent to admin for approval, and the order screen shows the request as "pending" until admin decides.

Request a refund or raise a complaint within the allowed period (Ref: Req #46)

  1. Open a delivered order from "My Orders".
  2. Look for the "Return & Refund" section on the Order Details screen (it shows a countdown of how much time is left to request one).
  3. Alternatively, tap the "Help" button on the order and choose "Items missing or incorrect" or "Item quality issue".
  4. Fill in the reason and submit your return/refund request.
✅ Expected Result: Your request is submitted and the order shows a return/refund status (e.g. "Requested" / "Under review"), and once approved, a "Refund Successful" message shows the amount credited to your wallet or cash.

Accept or reject a replacement suggested by the seller (Ref: Req #47)

  1. When a seller has run out of an item and proposes a replacement, open the affected order from "My Orders" — you will see a "Replacement approval required" card.
  2. Review the suggested alternative item(s) shown.
  3. Tap "Approve" to accept the replacement, or "Reject replacement" to decline it.
✅ Expected Result: If approved, the order updates with the new item and price. If rejected, the seller is notified and the original item is not swapped, with the order price unaffected.

Reorder a previous order (Ref: Req #48)

  1. Go to "My Orders" and find any order marked "Delivered".
  2. Tap the "Buy Again" button on that order (available from the orders list or the Order Details screen).
  3. Go to your Cart and check the items.
✅ Expected Result: The same items from that past order are added to your Cart, ready for you to check out again.

Rate and give feedback on a delivered order (Ref: Req #49)

  1. Open a "Delivered" order from "My Orders".
  2. Scroll to the "Rate your order" section listing the items you bought.
  3. Tap the star rating for an item, optionally type a short comment, and submit.
✅ Expected Result: A confirmation such as "Thanks for rating [item]!" appears, and your rating is saved against that product as a verified purchase.

Add a seller/store to favourites (Ref: Req #50)

  1. Open any store's page (tap a store card from Home or Stores).
  2. Tap the "Favorite" button (heart icon) near the store name.
  3. Go to your Favourites/Favourite Stores screen from the profile menu to confirm.
✅ Expected Result: The heart button changes to "Unlike" and shows the store as favourited, and the store now appears in your Favourite Stores list.

View your previous orders (Ref: Req #51)

  1. Open the menu/profile and tap "My Orders" (or "Orders").
  2. Scroll through the list of past and current orders.
  3. Tap any order to view its full details.
✅ Expected Result: All your past orders are listed with date, store, item count, total amount, and status, and each one opens to show full details.

17.1 Store / Shop Section in Customer App

This section covers how store cards are shown to customers when browsing stores — logos, banners, ratings, and delivery time estimates.

Premium-looking store cards (Ref: Req #172)

  1. Open the "Stores" section from the Home screen or bottom navigation.
  2. Look at how each store is presented in the list — its card design, spacing, and images.
✅ Expected Result: Store cards look polished and modern, with a clear image, name, rating, and delivery time — not a plain text list.

Store logo shown on the store card (Ref: Req #173)

  1. On the "Stores" listing screen, look at the small image/icon on each store card.
✅ Expected Result: Each store's own logo image is shown on its card (falling back to a neutral placeholder only if the seller has not uploaded a logo).

Store banners (Ref: Req #174)

  1. Open an individual store's page by tapping its card.
  2. Look at the large banner image at the top of the store page (it may rotate automatically if the store has more than one banner).
✅ Expected Result: The store's banner image(s) appear at the top of its store page. (Note: this banner currently appears on the store's own page, not on the smaller store card in the Stores list.)

Ratings display on store cards (Ref: Req #175)

  1. On the "Stores" listing screen, check each card for a star/number rating.
✅ Expected Result: A rating out of 5 (e.g. "4.5/5") is shown on each store card that has received ratings.

Delivery time estimate display (Ref: Req #176)

  1. On the "Stores" listing screen, check each card for an estimated delivery time (e.g. "10-15 mins").
✅ Expected Result: A delivery time estimate is visible on every store card.

Modern animations and gradient styling (Ref: Req #177)

  1. Browse the Stores list and an individual store page, paying attention to colours, transitions, and loading effects (e.g. banners fading in, buttons highlighting on tap).
✅ Expected Result: The store browsing screens use smooth colour gradients and subtle animation effects rather than a flat, static look.

17.2 Shop Module Details

This section covers the richer content shown on an individual store's own page — video, banners, and its story.

Video banner on the shop page (Ref: Req #178)

  1. Open a store's page that you know has added a promotional video (look for a "Store Spotlight" section).
  2. Check whether the video plays inline on the page.
✅ Expected Result: If the seller has added a video, a "Store Spotlight" video player appears on their store page and plays correctly.

Shop page banner / YouTube video display (Ref: Req #179)

  1. Open a store page and scroll through the top section.
  2. Check for a "Sponsored" advertisement image and, separately, for an embedded YouTube video (via the "Store Spotlight" section if the seller has linked a YouTube video).
✅ Expected Result: Sponsored advertisement banners appear where relevant, and a YouTube video plays correctly if the store has one linked. (Note: a general decorative "shop banner" image separate from the advertisement banner is not yet available — only the top rotating store banner and the video spotlight exist today.)

Signature products section on the shop page (Ref: Req #180)

  1. Open a store's page that has marked some products as "signature" items.
  2. Scroll down to look for a "Signature Products" section with a star icon.
✅ Expected Result: A "Signature Products" section appears showing the store's highlighted/best products in a distinct styled block.

"About My Shop" section (Ref: Req #181)

  1. Open any store's page.
  2. Scroll to the "About My Shop" heading.
✅ Expected Result: A section titled "About My Shop" is visible, showing the store's own description written by the seller.

17.3 Store Model Requirements

This section covers the underlying store information — details, ratings, hours, and delivery coverage — and how quickly stores are expected to deliver.

Store details, ratings, operating hours, and delivery radius are all tracked (Ref: Req #182)

  1. Open a store page and look for its shop name, description, rating, and any shown operating hours.
  2. Try viewing the same store while set to a delivery address that is far away, and again from an address close to the store, to see whether availability changes based on distance.
✅ Expected Result: The store page shows complete store information (name, description, rating), and stores that are outside your delivery area do not appear as deliverable to you, confirming a delivery radius is being applied.

Delivery target of 12-15 minutes (Ref: Req #183)

How this is confirmed: This is a target service speed, not a single on-screen switch. Check it by placing a few real test orders from nearby stores and comparing the time estimate shown at checkout (and the actual delivery time) against the 12-15 minute goal — this should be reviewed with the Zinto team as a performance target rather than a single pass/fail click-test, since the final delivery-time promise has not yet been locked in.

19. Advertisement & Promotional Visibility Module

This section covers the different types of banners and ads a customer sees while browsing.

Banners on the homepage (Ref: Req #191)

  1. Open the Home screen.
  2. Look at the large rotating banner carousel near the top.
✅ Expected Result: One or more promotional banner images are shown at the top of the Home screen and rotate automatically.

Banners inside promotional sections (Ref: Req #192)

  1. Scroll further down the Home screen past the top banner, through the category/offer sections.
  2. Look for additional banner images placed between product sections.
✅ Expected Result: Additional promotional banners appear within the page as you scroll, not only at the very top.

Banners for store/shop visibility (Ref: Req #193)

  1. Browse Home and the Stores list, watching for any banner specifically promoting a particular store.
✅ Expected Result: Banners that promote a specific store are visible to customers browsing the app.

Banners that redirect somewhere when tapped (Ref: Req #194)

  1. Tap any promotional banner on the Home screen.
✅ Expected Result: Tapping the banner takes you to the relevant destination — a product, a store, an offers page, or an external link, depending on how that banner was set up.

Super Ad / premium advertisement placement (Ref: Req #195)

  1. Scroll through the Home screen looking for a strip of images labelled "Sponsored" in a small tag on the image.
  2. Tap one of these "Sponsored" items.
✅ Expected Result: A "Sponsored" advertisement strip is visible among the regular sections, and tapping an ad takes you to the seller's page or product it points to.

20. Terms & Conditions / Consent Requirements

This section covers where customers are asked to agree to the platform's rules.

Mandatory Terms & Conditions acceptance when signing up (Ref: Req #196)

  1. Go to the login screen and choose to sign up as a new customer.
  2. Fill in your details and try tapping "Continue" without ticking the Terms & Conditions checkbox.
  3. Then tick the checkbox and try again.
✅ Expected Result: You cannot complete sign-up until you tick the box agreeing to the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy; a message reminds you to agree if you try to skip it.

Mandatory Terms & Conditions acceptance at checkout (Ref: Req #197)

  1. Add an item to your cart and go to Checkout.
  2. Try to place the order without ticking the "I agree to the Return & Exchange Policy" checkbox.
  3. Then tick the checkbox and place the order.
✅ Expected Result: The order cannot be placed until the policy checkbox is ticked; once ticked, the order goes through normally.

"Accept & Agree" checkbox required everywhere consent is needed (Ref: Req #198)

  1. Repeat the sign-up test (Req #196) and the checkout test (Req #197) above.
  2. Check whether any other screen in the app asks you to agree to a policy (for example, when adding a payment method) and confirm a checkbox is present there too.
✅ Expected Result: Every place in the app that requires you to accept a policy shows a clear checkbox that must be ticked before you can continue.

21.1 Login / Signup Page

This section covers the look, feel, and methods available for signing in.

Modern authentication screen design (Ref: Req #199)

  1. Open the Zinto app while logged out and view the login/sign-up screen.
✅ Expected Result: The login screen has a clean, modern design with clear category imagery, smooth transitions between login and sign-up, and easy-to-read fields.

OTP verification during login (Ref: Req #200)

  1. On the login screen, enter your mobile number and tap "Send OTP" / "Continue".
  2. Enter the 4-digit code you receive and tap to verify.
✅ Expected Result: An OTP is sent and, once entered correctly (within the time shown by the countdown), you are logged in.

Login using email (Ref: Req #201)

  1. On the login screen, tap the option to switch to "Login with Email instead".
  2. Enter your email address and tap "Send OTP".
  3. Enter the OTP received on your email and continue.
✅ Expected Result: You can complete login using your email address and an OTP sent to that email, as an alternative to mobile number login.

Login using mobile number (Ref: Req #202)

  1. On the login screen (default view), enter your mobile number.
  2. Complete the OTP step as usual.
✅ Expected Result: You can log in successfully using just your mobile number and OTP.

Responsive, mobile-first design (Ref: Req #203)

  1. Open the Zinto app on a phone (or resize a browser window to a narrow phone-sized width).
  2. Browse the Home screen, a store page, the Cart, and Checkout.
  3. Repeat on a tablet or desktop-sized screen if available.
✅ Expected Result: All screens display cleanly and remain usable at phone width first, then adapt sensibly on larger screens, with no overlapping text or cut-off buttons.

21.2 Home Page

This section covers the key building blocks of the Home screen customers see first.

Search bar on the Home screen (Ref: Req #204)

  1. Open the Home screen and locate the search bar near the top.
  2. Tap it and type a product name.
✅ Expected Result: The search bar is clearly visible at the top of the Home screen and opens the search results screen when used.

Delivery location shown and changeable (Ref: Req #205)

  1. Look at the top of the Home screen for your current delivery address/location.
  2. Tap it to open the location picker and change your delivery address.
✅ Expected Result: Your current delivery location is shown at the top of Home, and tapping it lets you pick or search for a new address, updating what is shown as deliverable.

Promotional banners on Home (Ref: Req #206)

  1. Look at the rotating banner carousel on the Home screen.
✅ Expected Result: Promotional banner images are shown and automatically rotate every few seconds.

Trending products section (Ref: Req #207)

  1. Scroll down the Home screen to find a "Trending" products section.
✅ Expected Result: A "Trending Products" section is visible with a horizontally scrollable list of popular items.

Recommended stores section (Ref: Req #208)

  1. Scroll down the Home screen looking for a "Recommended Stores" section.
✅ Expected Result: A "Recommended Stores" section appears on the Home screen showing stores suggested for you.

Offers / recommended offers section (Ref: Req #209)

  1. Scroll through Home to find any "Offers" section, or open the dedicated "Offers" screen from the menu.
✅ Expected Result: Current offers/discounted products are displayed, either as a Home screen section or on a dedicated Offers page.

21.3 Search Features

This section covers the different ways a customer can search for what they want.

Text search (Ref: Req #210)

  1. Tap the search bar and type a product name (e.g. "bread").
✅ Expected Result: Matching results appear as you type or after tapping search.

Voice search (Ref: Req #211)

  1. On the search screen, tap the microphone icon inside the search bar.
  2. Allow microphone access if prompted, then say a product name out loud.
✅ Expected Result: The app listens (shown with a pulsing microphone animation), converts your speech to text, and shows matching search results.

Search by store (Ref: Req #212)

  1. On the search screen, type the name of a known store.
✅ Expected Result: The matching store appears in the search results and can be opened directly.

Search by category (Ref: Req #213)

  1. Open the "Categories" screen from Home or the bottom navigation.
  2. Tap a category (e.g. "Grocery") and check the products shown.
  3. Optionally, use the subcategory tabs shown on that category's page to narrow results further.
✅ Expected Result: Selecting a category (and any subcategory) shows only products belonging to it.

21.4 Attractive Store Section

This section covers the overall look and discoverability of the store browsing experience.

Store section with premium UI and strong discoverability (Ref: Req #214)

  1. Open the "Stores" section from Home or the bottom navigation.
  2. Check that stores are easy to filter/sort (for example, by nearest, top rated, or category) and that the visual design feels premium.
✅ Expected Result: Stores are easy to find and browse, with clear categorisation/filters and an attractive, well-organised layout.

21.5 Client UI Expectations

This section covers overall look-and-feel and performance expectations across the app.

Premium overall UI quality (Ref: Req #215)

  1. Browse through Home, a product page, Cart, and Checkout, paying attention to fonts, colours, spacing, and imagery.
✅ Expected Result: The app feels consistent, modern, and polished throughout, not rough or unfinished.

Search is fast and intuitive (Ref: Req #216)

  1. Use the search bar to search for a common product.
  2. Note how quickly results appear and how easy the screen is to use.
✅ Expected Result: Search results appear quickly (within a second or two) and the results screen is easy to understand at a glance.

Store browsing is highly attractive (Ref: Req #217)

  1. Spend a minute scrolling through the Stores list and a couple of individual store pages.
✅ Expected Result: Store browsing feels visually engaging with good use of images, banners, and layout — this is a design-quality check, so use your own judgement on whether it looks appealing.

33.1 Feedback & Rating

This section covers a customer's ability to leave feedback and ratings after an order is delivered.

Customer can provide order feedback (Ref: Req #355)

  1. Open a "Delivered" order from "My Orders".
  2. Scroll to the "Rate your order" section and, for any item, tap to expand the comment box.
  3. Type a short comment about the product and submit it along with a star rating.
✅ Expected Result: Your written comment is submitted successfully alongside your star rating, with a confirmation message shown.

Customer can provide a rating / review (Ref: Req #356)

  1. Open a "Delivered" order from "My Orders".
  2. In the "Rate your order" section, tap the stars (1 to 5) next to any item to set a rating.
  3. Submit the rating.
✅ Expected Result: The star rating is saved against that product, marked as a verified purchase, and a "Thanks for rating" confirmation appears. If you already rated that product before, you instead see "You've already rated [item]."

33.2 Reorder

This section confirms customers can quickly repeat a past order.

Customer can reorder a previously placed order (Ref: Req #357)

  1. Go to "My Orders" and find a "Delivered" order.
  2. Tap "Buy Again" on that order.
  3. Open your Cart to confirm the same items were added.
✅ Expected Result: All items from the original order (that are still available) are added straight to your Cart, ready to check out again without re-searching for each product.

33.3 Favourite Seller

This section confirms customers can save and revisit preferred stores.

Customer can add a seller/store to favourites (Ref: Req #358)

  1. Open any store's page.
  2. Tap the heart-shaped "Favorite" button next to the store name.
  3. Go to your profile menu and open "Favourite Stores".
  4. Confirm the store you favourited is listed, then try removing it by tapping "Unlike".
✅ Expected Result: The store is added to (and can be removed from) your Favourite Stores list, and the favourite/unlike button state updates immediately on the store page.

33.4 Previous Orders

This section confirms customers can review their full order history.

Customer can view previous orders / order history (Ref: Req #359)

  1. Open the profile/menu and tap "My Orders".
  2. Scroll through the full list of orders, both past and current.
  3. Tap on any past order to view its full item list, pricing, and status history.
✅ Expected Result: Every order you have ever placed is listed and can be opened to see its full details, regardless of how long ago it was placed.
